About the Studio

AppMakerStudio is an independent Android development studio focused on building tools that are fast, minimal, and privacy-respecting. Every app is designed to do one thing well — no bloat, no unnecessary permissions, and processing kept on-device wherever possible.

What started as a single scanning app has grown into a small suite of everyday utilities — document scanning, text tools, and unit conversion — each built and refined based on real user feedback rather than shipped once and forgotten.
