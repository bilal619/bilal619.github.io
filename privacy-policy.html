Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Information We Collect

Our applications do not require user accounts and do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Content you create in-app — such as scanned documents, saved text, or conversion history — is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Usage Data & Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Google Play Console to understand app usage, such as active users, session length, feature engagement, and crash reports. This data is anonymized and aggregated, and is used only to improve app performance and stability. It is not linked to your identity.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may display advertisements through Google AdMob and use Firebase Analytics, both provided by Google. These services may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, and usage data as described in Google's privacy policy. We do not control the data collected by third-party ad or analytics networks beyond what we configure.

Data Storage & Security

All user-generated content (scan history, saved text, preferences) is stored exclusively on your device. We do not maintain servers or databases that store your personal content. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact me at theappmakers.pk@gmail.com. We will respond to inquiries within a reasonable timeframe.
